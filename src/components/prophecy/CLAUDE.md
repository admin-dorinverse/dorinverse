# prophecy

Pieces of the Prophecy and Fulfillment pages: one half of a link (held, or still to find in stardust) and a link's
card on the list. The count and filter chips are shared, in `common/`.
Pages get cookie data only through src/data/queries/cookies.ts. A half still to find shows only its reference: its
words are never on the phone, so its stardust is a made-up shape. Joined cards glow softly and never animate.

# connections

Pieces of Character Connections (the web, opened from its Collections card): the draggable web drawn once into a
picture, its top bar, link filter and zoom buttons, and the person and link pages' small webs, lists and passages. Everyone and their spots come from
`src/data/characterWebLayout.ts` and every link from `characterWebLinks.ts`, both
made by `scripts/character-web/layout.mjs`.

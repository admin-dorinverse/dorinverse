# bakery/tv

Pieces of the Bakery TV page: the wooden TV with what is playing now, one guide row with its "Remind me" button,
the sums that work out what plays when, and a once-a-second clock that stops while the page is hidden.
The programs are sample data (`src/data/sample/bakeryTv.ts`) until the app loads `tv_program` / `tv_schedule`.
Reminders only show on screen for now; nothing is saved or sent.

# Trivia

Trivia questions are true or false and multiple choice questions a baker collects from lessons. The app can ask any of them at random, so each one must stand on its own.

They come from the Prep walkthrough's choice cards. Each question keeps its lesson (passage) and its verses, so a baker collects it by finishing that lesson.

## The rule

**Every question must make sense when asked at random, with no lesson before it.** Say who and what is happening in plain words: use a person's name when the lesson's verses give it; otherwise describe them (for example, "the first man and woman"). Never use a book, chapter or verse number in the question. Never start with "Then", "Now" or "Next", and never use "he", "she" or "they" before saying who it is. Keep the same right answer, and only use facts from the lesson's verses.

Examples:
- Prep card: "Then God tells the people what He has given them for food. What is it?"
  Trivia: "After God made the first man and woman, what did He give them for food?"
- Prep card: "Then God does something with the seventh day itself. What is it?"
  Trivia: "After God finished making everything, what did He do with the seventh day?"
- Prep card: "The family leaves Bethlehem to go live in the land of ___."
  Trivia: "During a famine, Naomi's family left Bethlehem to live in which land?"

## Which questions

- **3 to 4 main questions per passage:** the big event, the key people, and what God did or said. Not small details. Any question type is fine; there is no set mix.
- Only the passage's own choice cards are used. No new questions are made up. A passage with only 3 choice cards keeps all 3.
- **Fill in the blank becomes multiple choice,** with the same 3 answers. A two-blank card is used only if it reads well as one question with 3 answers; otherwise pick another card.

## How they read

- **Short and easy to read, about 15 to 20 words.** One question, one point. A true or false statement tests one claim.
- The Prep walkthrough's rules still apply: no em dashes or en dashes to make a sentence longer, no hard word the choice hangs on, the question never holds its own answer, and never ask the baker to guess the next words of a line.
- **Answers follow the Prep answer rules:** 3 answers for multiple choice, 2 for true or false; no commas or periods; all about the same length and tone; no near-matches; each wrong answer is false anywhere in the passage. Keep the card's answers unless one breaks a rule.
- No Papa Dorin voice. Plain words, the BSB's names.

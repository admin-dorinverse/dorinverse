# Station: Parables

This file covers the content for the Parables station.

## What it is

A friend heard the parable and didn't get it. The friend asks the baker about it, and the baker explains, using only what the text says. Every question has 3 answers.

## The passage text

- The passage text is provided in the **BSB** (Berean Standard Bible).
- Every true fact and every verse number comes from the BSB.

## Which parable

- **One parable per station.**
- **Only a parable the text explains.** Either Jesus explains it himself, like the sower (Matthew 13:18–23), or the text states its point, like the lost sheep: "I tell you that in the same way there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents" (Luke 15:7).
- **A parable the text doesn't explain can't be used.** For example, the hidden treasure (Matthew 13:44) never says what the treasure is or who the man is. Any answer would be an interpretation, not the text.
- **More than one explained parable in the passage:** use the one the text explains most fully. The others are told in Prep.
- If the passage has no explained parable, it gets no Parables station.

## The friend

- The friend asks every question.
- The friend sounds like a friend who really wants to understand: curious, asking because they want to get it.
- **Written for adults.** No scenarios about school, parents or anything aimed at kids. Kids can still play.

## The questions

- Ask as many as it takes for the friend to understand the parable and for it to make sense.
- Every right answer comes from one of these:
  - the text's own explanation of the parable
  - what happens in the parable itself
  - the verses right around the parable that say who it was told to or what it was about
- Never add a meaning the text doesn't state.

## Answer rules

- Each question has **3 answers** and **one try**.
- All three answers are the same kind of thing, about the same length and the same tone. None are silly.
- Each wrong answer must be false for that moment.
- The code shuffles where the right answer sits. The agent doesn't need to.

## Output for each question

- The question
- The 3 answers
- The right answer
- The verse that backs it

## Cookies

- Every right answer adds **1 cinnamon cookie** to the box.
- A wrong answer adds nothing.

## Example: the two builders (Matthew 7:24–27)

1. **Friend:** "I didn't get the house story. Who's the wise builder supposed to be?"
   - A. Everyone who has the money to build well
   - B. Everyone who goes to the temple faithfully
   - **C. Everyone who hears Jesus' words and acts on them** *(7:24)*
2. **Friend:** "And the foolish one?"
   - **A. Everyone who hears His words but doesn't act on them** *(7:26)*
   - B. Everyone who never gets to hear His words
   - C. Everyone who builds in too much of a hurry
3. **Friend:** "Did the storm skip the wise man's house?"
   - A. Yes, the rock kept the storm away
   - **B. No, the same storm beat against both houses** *(7:25, 7:27)*
   - C. Yes, the storm only hit the sand
4. **Friend:** "So why did one house stand?"
   - A. It was built bigger and stronger
   - B. The wise man repaired it mid-storm
   - **C. Its foundation was on the rock** *(7:25)*
5. **Friend:** "What words is He talking about?"
   - **A. The things He had just finished saying** *(7:24, 7:28)*
   - B. Old sayings passed down by the scribes
   - C. Instructions for building houses
6. **Friend:** "So just hearing His words isn't enough?"
   - A. Hearing is enough if you remember it
   - **B. Right. You have to act on what you hear** *(7:24, 7:26)*
   - C. Acting is enough even if you never hear

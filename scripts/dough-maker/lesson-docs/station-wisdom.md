# Station: Wisdom

This file covers the content for the Wisdom station.

## What it is

Papa Dorin gives the baker everyday scenarios, one at a time. For each one, the baker decides: **wise** or **foolish**. After the pick, the verse behind it shows, with its reference. That verse is where the wisdom comes from.

## The passage text

- The passage text is provided in the **BSB** (Berean Standard Bible).
- Every verse shown is the exact BSB wording, with its reference.

## Which sayings can be used

- The Prep story cards and choice cards for the passage are provided.
- **Only sayings Prep taught can be used.** Every scenario comes from a saying Papa Dorin told or asked about in Prep.
- **Each saying states a result.** It says where the wise way leads, where the foolish way leads, or both. A verse that doesn't state a result can't be used.

## Who's talking

- Papa Dorin presents every scenario. His voice comes from his own file.
- **Written for adults.** No scenarios about school, parents or anything aimed at kids. Kids can still play.

## The scenarios

- **5 to 7 scenarios** per passage.
- Each scenario is a short, everyday situation, made up and set in modern life. It is not taken from the passage.
- Each scenario applies one saying.
- **Clear-cut.** The saying decides it. No gray areas, and nothing a thoughtful baker could argue either way.
- **A mix** of wise and foolish.

## Answer rules

- Every scenario has **2 answers: Wise or Foolish**, and **one try**.

## Output for each scenario

- The scenario
- The right answer: Wise or Foolish
- The verse that shows after the pick, in exact BSB wording, with its reference

## Cookies

- Every right answer adds **1 honey cookie** to the box.
- A wrong answer adds nothing.

## Example: Proverbs 10

These examples assume Prep taught each of these sayings.

1. **Scenario:** "You keep putting off the side project you said you'd finish, and you scroll your phone instead."
   - **Foolish**
   - Shows after: "Idle hands make one poor, but diligent hands bring wealth." *(Proverbs 10:4)*
2. **Scenario:** "The cashier gives you too much change. You go back and return it."
   - **Wise**
   - Shows after: "He who walks in integrity walks securely, but he who perverts his ways will be found out." *(Proverbs 10:9)*
3. **Scenario:** "A friend snapped at you last week and apologized. You let it go instead of bringing it up again."
   - **Wise**
   - Shows after: "Hatred stirs up dissension, but love covers all transgressions." *(Proverbs 10:12)*
4. **Scenario:** "Your manager points out a mistake in your report. You brush it off and keep doing it the same way."
   - **Foolish**
   - Shows after: "Whoever heeds instruction is on the path to life, but he who ignores reproof goes astray." *(Proverbs 10:17)*
5. **Scenario:** "In a heated meeting, you hold back and think before you speak."
   - **Wise**
   - Shows after: "When words are many, sin is unavoidable, but he who restrains his lips is wise." *(Proverbs 10:19)*

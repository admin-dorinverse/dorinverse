# Weekly Word

One mystery word a week. A clue comes each day, Monday to Sunday, and the word is revealed Sunday evening. A baker gets 3 guesses a day.

5 years of weeks are written ahead of time, about 260 words. The word list comes first: all the words are made and looked over before any clues are written.

## The word

- **One word a baker can spell and type,** like "manna", "ark", "Goliath" or "Passover". It can be a thing, a person, a place or an event.
- **It is in the BSB,** spelled the way the BSB spells it.
- **Only one thing fits it.** Skip a name many people share, like "John" or "Mary", unless the clues make it clear which one.
- **Well known enough to guess by Sunday.** Skip a word only Bible scholars would know.
- **No repeats across all 5 years.** Mix the kinds week to week, so it isn't a person three weeks in a row.
- **Accepted answers:** list every way to type it that should count: plural, "the" in front, other spellings (for example "Noah's ark", "ark").

## The clues

- **7 clues, all medium hard.** Each one points to the word, but no single clue gives it away. The order doesn't matter.
- **Each clue gives enough setting to place it in the Bible:** who, where or when. A clue that could be about almost anything is too vague.
  - ❌ "It showed up in the morning, when the dew was gone."
  - ✅ "In the wilderness, God's people found it on the ground each morning after the dew lifted."
- **Each clue stands on its own.** A missed clue is gone for good, so no clue can lean on an earlier one ("It also…", "After that…").
- **Every clue comes from the BSB** and keeps its reference. The reference is shown only after the reveal.
- **The clue never holds the word,** part of it, or a form of it ("manna" can't be in a clue, and neither can "man").
- **No book, chapter or verse number** in the clue's words.
- **All 7 together fit only this word.** Read them all: if another word fits too, change a clue.
- **Short and plain,** about 10 to 20 words, one point each. No Papa Dorin voice. No em dashes.

## Checking

A separate checker reads the word, its accepted answers, all 7 clues and their BSB verses. It checks each rule above, and that every clue is true to its verse.

# Main characters

_Written by `node scripts/dough-maker/roster.mjs`. Edit the script, not this file._

These are the characters a cookie box can reveal. Once saved to the database the list stands on its own, so changing a lesson title or reloading the Bible data never changes who is on it.

Drafted from everyone the lessons lean on (403 people): those with at least two of three signs (a title names them; scenes in 2+ chapters, not counting genealogies; a dictionary entry of their own), plus the people you added. God, the Holy Spirit, Satan, angels and false gods are left off. Jesus is listed with the Gospels.

**Flags** in the last column:

- **one chapter — check for 2 moments**: every verse that names them is in one chapter.
- **no place named with them**: their places will be picked by hand.
- **also a nation or tribe**: their verse count includes verses about the nation, so it runs high.

When two people share a name, the row says where the Bible first names this one.

**233 characters**, 36 of them added by you (marked _added_).

How to read an entry:
- **Title** — after the book name, what the player learns from the whole flavor, as this recipe teaches and tests it (2.10).
- **Range** — the verses the flavor teaches and reveals (Question Rules 2.12 format).
- **Parallel** — other accounts of the same events: revealed after the base, never a question source (2.17).
- **Frame** — verses outside the range that lesson 1 also draws on (Letters: who wrote it, to whom, and why, 7.L2).
- **Lessons** — one numbered line per lesson: its range, then its title in plain words (2.10). The line under it says what the lesson teaches toward the flavor title.
- **Split option** — where the flavor could become two flavors that each still have 2–5 lessons (2.4), or "None".
- **People / Places** — who and where the reference layer links to the range, most-mentioned first.
- **Turn** — the words in the text where the section begins.

## Romans

### 1. Romans: The Good News Paul Preached
- **Range:** Romans 1:1–17
- **Lessons:** 2
  1. Romans 1:1–7 — Paul and His Message
     - Paul says who he is, who he writes to, and that he was set apart for God’s good news about His Son (1:1–4).
  2. Romans 1:8–17 — The Power of the Gospel
     - What the good news is: God’s power to save everyone who believes (1:16–17). Paul’s wish to visit Rome is folded in with no questions (2.14).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (4):** God, Jesus Christ, David, Paul
- **Places (1):** Rome
- **Turn:** Opening greeting: "Paul, a servant of Jesus Christ… To all that be in Rome" (1:1, 1:7), then "I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ" (1:16).

### 2. Romans: All Stand Guilty Before God
- **Range:** Romans 1:18–3:20
- **Frame:** Romans 1:1–7
- **Lessons:** 4
  1. Romans 1:18–32 — People Who Turned Away from God
     - The first group Paul shows guilty: people who knew God but did not honour Him (1:21).
  2. Romans 2:1–16 — God Judges Everyone Fairly
     - Those who judge others are guilty too, because God judges each person by what they have done (2:1, 2:6).
  3. Romans 2:17–29 — Having God’s Law Is Not Enough
     - The Jews, who had the law, are guilty too when they break it (2:23).
  4. Romans 3:1–20 — No One Is Righteous
     - The argument’s end: “all the world” is guilty before God, and the law cannot make anyone right (3:19–20).
- **Split option:** At 2:17 (2 + 2 lessons).
- **People (2):** God, Jesus Christ
- **Places (0):** —
- **Turn:** "For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men" (1:18).

### 3. Romans: Made Right with God by Faith
- **Range:** Romans 3:21–5:21
- **Frame:** Romans 1:1–7
- **Lessons:** 5
  1. Romans 3:21–31 — God’s Way to Make People Right
     - The answer to the last flavor: God makes people right through faith in Jesus Christ, for all who believe (3:22).
  2. Romans 4:1–12 — How Abraham Was Made Right
     - The proof case: Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him for righteousness (4:3).
  3. Romans 4:13–25 — A Promise Received by Faith
     - The promise to Abraham came through faith, not the law, and is counted to all who believe (4:13, 4:24).
  4. Romans 5:1–11 — Peace with God
     - What those made right by faith now have: peace with God and hope (5:1–2).
  5. Romans 5:12–21 — One Man’s Sin, One Man’s Gift
     - How one man’s obedience makes many righteous, as one man’s sin brought death (5:12, 5:19).
- **Split option:** At 4:13 (2 + 3 lessons); or at 5:1 (3 + 2 lessons).
- **People (8):** God, Jesus Christ, Abraham, Adam, David, Holy Spirit, Moses, Sarah
- **Places (0):** —
- **Turn:** "But now the righteousness of God without the law is manifested" (3:21).

### 5. Romans: Freed by the Spirit, Not the Law

## 1 Corinthians

- **Range:** 1 Corinthians 1–2
- **Lessons:** 4

     - The problem the cross answers: divisions over Paul, Apollos and Cephas, and Paul refusing to preach “with wisdom of words” (1:12, 1:17).
  3. 1 Corinthians 1:18–31 — Why the Cross Seems Foolish
     - The cross is foolishness to the world but God’s power and wisdom, so no one may glory except in the Lord (1:18, 1:31).
  4. 1 Corinthians 2:1–16 — Wisdom Taught by God’s Spirit
     - Paul preached Christ crucified, and God’s wisdom is known only by His Spirit (2:2, 2:10).
- **Split option:** At 1:18 (2 + 2 lessons).
- **People (11):** God, Jesus Christ, Holy Spirit, Paul, Apollos, Chloe, Crispus, Gaius, Simon Peter, Sosthenes, Stephanas
- **Places (1):** Corinth
- **Turn:** Opening greeting: "Paul, called to be an apostle… and Sosthenes our brother, Unto the church of God which is at Corinth" (1:1–2).
- **Notes:** Co-sender Sosthenes is named (1:1). The "why" (contentions "declared unto me… by them which are of the house of Chloe", 1:11) sits in lesson 2; lesson 1 (1:1–9) has who and to whom but little "why".

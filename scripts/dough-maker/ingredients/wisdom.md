## Wisdom

### 1. Wisdom: When Wisdom Calls
- **Range:** Proverbs 1:1–19; Proverbs 1:20–33; Proverbs 2
- **Lessons:** 3
  1. Proverbs 1:1–19 — Do Not Consent to Sinners
     - The proverbs of Solomon are written “To know wisdom and instruction”, for “The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge”; when sinners say “Come with us”, a son is told, “consent thou not” (Proverbs 1:1–2, 1:7, 1:10–11).
  2. Proverbs 1:20–33 — Wisdom Cries in the Streets
     - “Wisdom crieth without”, asking the simple how long they will love simplicity; those who refuse her “eat of the fruit of their own way”, but “whoso hearkeneth unto me shall dwell safely” (Proverbs 1:20, 1:22, 1:31, 1:33).
  3. Proverbs 2 — Searching as for Hidden Treasure
     - Seek wisdom “as for hid treasures”, and you will find the knowledge of God, “For the LORD giveth wisdom”; it keeps you from the evil man and the strange woman (Proverbs 2:4–6, 2:12, 2:16).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (3):** God, David, Solomon
- **Places (0):** —
- **Turn:** Wisdom speaks aloud: “Wisdom crieth without; she uttereth her voice in the streets” (Proverbs 1:20).

### 2. Wisdom: Get Wisdom Above All
- **Range:** Proverbs 3:1–12; Proverbs 3:13–35; Proverbs 4:1–19
- **Lessons:** 3
  1. Proverbs 3:1–12 — Trusting the LORD Fully
     - “Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding”, honour Him with your substance, and do not despise His correction, “For whom the LORD loveth he correcteth” (Proverbs 3:5, 3:9, 3:11–12).
  2. Proverbs 3:13–35 — Happy Is Whoever Finds Wisdom
     - “Happy is the man that findeth wisdom”: she is “more precious than rubies” and “a tree of life”; so “Withhold not good from them to whom it is due” (Proverbs 3:13, 3:15, 3:18, 3:27).
  3. Proverbs 4:1–19 — The Path of the Just
     - A father passes on his father’s words: “Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom”; “the path of the just is as the shining light”, but the way of the wicked is darkness (Proverbs 4:4, 4:7, 4:18–19).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (1):** God
- **Places (0):** —
- **Turn:** The father says it plainly: “Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom” (Proverbs 4:7).

     - “Wisdom hath builded her house” and calls the simple to her table; “The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom”; the foolish woman also calls, but “her guests are in the depths of hell” (Proverbs 9:1, 9:4–5, 9:10, 9:13, 9:18).

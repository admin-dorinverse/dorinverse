How to read an entry:
- **Title** — after the book name, what the player learns from the whole flavor, as this recipe teaches and tests it (2.10).
- **Range** — the verses the flavor teaches and reveals (Question Rules 2.12 format).
- **Parallel** — other accounts of the same events: revealed after the base, never a question source (2.17).
- **Frame** — verses outside the range that lesson 1 also draws on (Letters: who wrote it, to whom, and why, 7.L2).
- **Lessons** — one numbered line per lesson: its range, then its title in plain words (2.10). The line under it says what the lesson teaches toward the flavor title.
- **Split option** — where the flavor could become two flavors that each still have 2–5 lessons (2.4), or "None".
- **People / Places** — who and where the reference layer links to the range, most-mentioned first.
- **Turn** — the words in the text where the section begins.

## Revelation

### 1. Revelation: Jesus Appears to John
- **Range:** Revelation 1
- **Lessons:** 2
  1. Revelation 1:1–8 — A Message from Jesus
     - Where the book comes from: God gave it to Jesus, who sent it by His angel to John, with the promise “Behold, he cometh with clouds” (1:1, 1:7).
  2. Revelation 1:9–20 — Jesus Among the Candlesticks
     - What John saw on Patmos: one like the Son of man among seven golden candlesticks, who explains that the candlesticks are the seven churches (1:12–13, 1:20).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (4):** God, Jesus Christ, John the Apostle, Holy Spirit
- **Places (9):** Asia, Ephesus, Laodicea, Patmos, Pergamum, Philadelphia, Sardis, Smyrna, Thyatira
- **Turn:** The book opens: "The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him" (1:1).

### 2. Revelation: Letters to Seven Churches
- **Range:** Revelation 2–3
- **Lessons:** 4
  1. Revelation 2:1–11 — To Ephesus and Smyrna
     - Jesus’ words to the first two churches: Ephesus has left its first love; Smyrna is told “be thou faithful unto death” (2:4, 2:10).
  2. Revelation 2:12–29 — To Pergamos and Thyatira
     - Pergamos holds fast His name but is told to repent of false teaching; Thyatira’s works are known, but it suffers “that woman Jezebel” to teach (2:13–16, 2:19–20).
  3. Revelation 3:1–13 — To Sardis and Philadelphia
     - Sardis is told to wake up; Philadelphia is given an open door that no man can shut (3:2, 3:8).
  4. Revelation 3:14–22 — To Laodicea
     - The last church is neither cold nor hot, and Jesus stands at the door and knocks (3:15, 3:20).
- **Split option:** At 3:1 (2 + 2 lessons).
- **People (10):** God, Holy Spirit, Satan, Antipas, Balaam, Balak, David, Jacob (Israel), Jesus Christ, Jezebel
- **Places (8):** Sardis, Thyatira, Ephesus, Jerusalem, Laodicea, Pergamum, Philadelphia, Smyrna
- **Turn:** "Unto the angel of the church of Ephesus write" (2:1).
- **Notes:** Seven letters in four lessons. Every letter ends “He that hath an ear, let him hear” with a promise “to him that overcometh”, so the lessons share one pattern. Titles name the churches because each letter is addressed by name.

### 3. Revelation: The Throne in Heaven
- **Range:** Revelation 4–5
- **Lessons:** 2
  1. Revelation 4 — Holy, Holy, Holy
     - A door is opened in heaven: one sits on the throne, and the elders and four beasts cry “Holy, holy, holy” (4:1–2, 4:8).
  2. Revelation 5 — The Lamb Takes the Book
     - No one can open the sealed book but the Lamb that was slain, and all of heaven worships Him (5:5–6, 5:12).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (3):** Jesus Christ, God, David
- **Places (0):** —
- **Turn:** "After this I looked, and, behold, a door was opened in heaven" (4:1).

### 4. Revelation: The Lamb Opens Six Seals
- **Range:** Revelation 6
- **Lessons:** 2

     - As the Lamb opens the first four seals, John sees white, red, black, and pale horses and their riders (6:1–8).
  2. Revelation 6:9–17 — The Fifth and Sixth Seals
     - Souls under the altar cry “How long?”, then a great earthquake, and people hide from the wrath of the Lamb (6:10, 6:12, 6:16).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (2):** Jesus Christ, God
- **Places (0):** —
- **Turn:** "And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seals" (6:1).

### 5. Revelation: The Servants of God
- **Range:** Revelation 7
- **Lessons:** 2
  1. Revelation 7:1–8 — The Servants Are Sealed
     - Four angels hold back the winds until God’s servants are sealed in their foreheads, 12,000 from each tribe (7:1–4).
  2. Revelation 7:9–17 — A Great Multitude in White
     - A multitude no man could number stands before the throne in white robes, and an elder tells John who they are (7:9, 7:13–15).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (13):** God, Jesus Christ, Asher, Benjamin (patriarch), Gad, Issachar, Jacob (Israel), Joseph (son of Jacob), Levi (patriarch), Manasseh (son of Joseph), Reuben, Simeon, Zebulun
- **Places (0):** —
- **Turn:** "And after these things I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth" (7:1).
- **Notes:** Both lessons show God’s servants: sealed on earth (7:3) and serving “day and night in his temple” (7:15).

### 6. Revelation: The Trumpets Sound
- **Range:** Revelation 8–9
- **Lessons:** 4
  1. Revelation 8:1–6 — Prayers Rise Before God
     - The seventh seal brings silence, and the prayers of the saints rise with incense before seven angels prepare to sound (8:1, 8:3–4, 8:6).
  2. Revelation 8:7–13 — The First Four Trumpets
     - Hail and fire, a burning mountain, a falling star, and a darkened sun strike a third part of the earth (8:7–12).
  3. Revelation 9:1–12 — Locusts from the Pit
     - At the fifth trumpet the bottomless pit is opened, and locusts hurt those without God’s seal (9:1–4).
  4. Revelation 9:13–21 — Four Angels Are Let Loose
     - At the sixth trumpet four angels are loosed with an army of horsemen, yet the rest of men repent not (9:14–16, 9:20).
- **Split option:** At 9:1 (2 + 2 lessons).
- **People (2):** God, Apollyon
- **Places (1):** Euphrates
- **Turn:** "And when he had opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven" (8:1).
- **Notes:** The seventh seal opens onto the seven trumpets (8:1–2), so it starts this flavor. The seventh trumpet waits until 11:15.

### 7. Revelation: Sent to Prophesy
- **Range:** Revelation 10:1–11:14
- **Lessons:** 2
  1. Revelation 10 — John Eats the Little Book
     - A mighty angel gives John a little book, sweet in his mouth and bitter in his belly: “Thou must prophesy again” (10:9–11).
  2. Revelation 11:1–14 — The Two Witnesses
     - God’s two witnesses prophesy, are killed, and stand up again after three days and a half (11:3, 11:7, 11:11).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (3):** God, Holy Spirit, Jesus Christ
- **Places (2):** Egypt, Sodom
- **Turn:** "And I saw another mighty angel come down from heaven, clothed with a cloud" (10:1).
- **Notes:** One idea: John is told to prophesy again (10:11), and the two witnesses prophesy (11:3). Ends at “The second woe is past” (11:14).

### 8. Revelation: The Kingdom of Our God
- **Range:** Revelation 11:15–12:17
- **Lessons:** 2
  1. Revelation 11:15–12:6 — Born to Rule All Nations
     - Voices say the kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord; then a woman clothed with the sun bears a child caught up to God (11:15, 12:1, 12:5).
  2. Revelation 12:7–17 — War in Heaven
     - Michael fights the dragon, which the text names as the Devil and Satan; he is cast out, and a voice says the kingdom of our God is come (12:7–10).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (4):** God, Jesus Christ, Michael, Satan
- **Places (0):** —
- **Turn:** "And the seventh angel sounded; and there were great voices in heaven" (11:15).
- **Notes:** Both lessons announce God’s kingdom (11:15, 12:10). Lesson 1 holds the seventh trumpet and the woman’s man child, “who was to rule all nations” (12:5); the war in heaven (12:7) starts lesson 2.

### 9. Revelation: Worship the One Who Made Heaven
- **Range:** Revelation 13–14
- **Lessons:** 4
  1. Revelation 13:1–10 — A Beast Rises from the Sea
     - The dragon gives a beast its power, and all whose names are not in the Lamb’s book of life worship it (13:2, 13:8).
  2. Revelation 13:11–18 — A Beast from the Earth
     - A second beast makes the earth worship the first and causes all to receive a mark; its number is 666 (13:12, 13:16–18).
  3. Revelation 14:1–13 — The Lamb on Mount Sion
     - The Lamb stands with the 144,000, and three angels call all people to fear God and worship Him (14:1, 14:6–7, 14:9).
  4. Revelation 14:14–20 — The Harvest of the Earth
     - One like the Son of man reaps the earth, and the vine is cast into the winepress of God’s wrath (14:14–16, 14:19).
- **Split option:** At 14:1 (2 + 2 lessons).
- **People (3):** God, Jesus Christ, Holy Spirit
- **Places (2):** Babylon, Mount Zion
- **Turn:** "And I stood upon the sand of the sea, and saw a beast rise up out of the sea" (13:1).
- **Notes:** One question runs through both chapters: who is worshipped. The beasts demand it (13:4, 13:12, 13:15); the angel calls all to “worship him that made heaven, and earth” (14:7). The title is the text’s own command (14:7). For the lesson writer: the text does not say who the beast or the number stands for, so the lessons do not either (7.R3).

### 10. Revelation: The Seven Last Plagues
- **Range:** Revelation 15–16
- **Lessons:** 3
  1. Revelation 15 — The Song of Moses and the Lamb
     - Seven angels hold the seven last plagues, and those who overcame sing “Great and marvellous are thy works” (15:1–3).
  2. Revelation 16:1–11 — Plagues on Land, Sea, and Sun
     - Vials poured on the earth, sea, rivers, sun, and the beast’s throne, and men blaspheme God (16:2–11).
  3. Revelation 16:12–21 — A Voice Says It Is Done
     - The sixth vial dries up the great river and kings are gathered to battle; at the seventh a voice says “It is done” (16:12–17).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (3):** God, Jesus Christ, Moses
- **Places (3):** Armageddon, Babylon, Euphrates
- **Turn:** "And I saw another sign in heaven, great and marvellous, seven angels having the seven last plagues" (15:1).

### 11. Revelation: Babylon the Great Has Fallen
- **Range:** Revelation 17–18
- **Lessons:** 4
  1. Revelation 17:1–6 — A Woman on a Scarlet Beast
     - An angel shows John a woman on a scarlet beast, with BABYLON THE GREAT written on her forehead (17:3, 17:5).
  2. Revelation 17:7–18 — The Angel Explains the Vision
     - The angel explains what John saw: the waters are peoples, and the woman is that great city (17:7, 17:15, 17:18).
  3. Revelation 18:1–8 — God’s People Called Out
     - An angel cries “Babylon the great is fallen”, and a voice calls God’s people to come out of her (18:2, 18:4).
  4. Revelation 18:9–24 — Kings and Merchants Mourn
     - The kings, merchants, and shipmasters weep over the city, while heaven is told to rejoice (18:9, 18:11, 18:17, 18:20).
- **Split option:** At 18:1 (2 + 2 lessons).
- **People (2):** God, Jesus Christ
- **Places (1):** Babylon
- **Turn:** "And there came one of the seven angels which had the seven vials" (17:1).
- **Notes:** The title is the text’s own words (18:2). For the lesson writer: Babylon is taught exactly as the text names and explains it, “MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT” (17:5) and “that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth” (17:18). The text does not say which city on a map it is, so the lessons do not either (7.R3, 7.R4).

### 13. Revelation: Thrones of Judgment
- **Range:** Revelation 20
- **Lessons:** 2
  1. Revelation 20:1–6 — A Thousand Years
     - An angel binds Satan for a thousand years, and those who did not worship the beast live and reign with Christ (20:2, 20:4).
  2. Revelation 20:7–15 — The Great White Throne
     - Satan is loosed and then cast into the lake of fire, and the dead are judged out of the books before a great white throne (20:7, 20:10–12).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (5):** God, Jesus Christ, Satan, Gog, Magog
- **Places (2):** Gog, Magog
- **Turn:** "And I saw an angel come down from heaven, having the key of the bottomless pit" (20:1).
- **Notes:** Thrones and judgment in both lessons (20:4, 20:11–12). For the lesson writer: the thousand years is taught only as John saw it, with no timeline (7.R4).

  3. Revelation 21:22–22:5 — The River of the Water of Life
     - No temple and no night, for God and the Lamb are its light, and the river of life flows from the throne (21:22–23, 22:1, 22:5).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (4):** God, Jesus Christ, Jacob (Israel), John the Apostle
- **Places (1):** Jerusalem
- **Turn:** "And I saw a new heaven and a new earth" (21:1).

### 15. Revelation: Jesus Will Come Quickly
- **Range:** Revelation 22:6–21
- **Lessons:** 2
  1. Revelation 22:6–15 — These Sayings Are True
     - The angel says these sayings are faithful and true, and Jesus says “Behold, I come quickly” (22:6–7, 22:12).
  2. Revelation 22:16–21 — The Spirit and Bride Say Come
     - Jesus sends His testimony to the churches; the Spirit and the bride say “Come”, and John answers “Even so, come, Lord Jesus” (22:16–17, 22:20).
- **Split option:** None. Any cut would leave a flavor with 1 lesson (2.4).
- **People (5):** God, Jesus Christ, David, Holy Spirit, John the Apostle
- **Places (0):** —
- **Turn:** "And he said unto me, These sayings are faithful and true" (22:6).
- **Notes:** Jesus’ coming is taught as a promise (7.R2), three times in these verses (22:7, 22:12, 22:20).

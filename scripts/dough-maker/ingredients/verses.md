How to read an entry:
- **Title** — after the book name, what the player learns from the whole flavor, as this recipe teaches and tests it (2.10).
- **Range** — the verses the flavor teaches and reveals (Question Rules 2.12 format).
- **Parallel** — other accounts of the same events: revealed after the base, never a question source (2.17).
- **Frame** — verses outside the range that lesson 1 also draws on (Letters: who wrote it, to whom, and why, 7.L2).
- **Lessons** — one numbered line per lesson: its range, then its title in plain words (2.10). The line under it says what the lesson teaches toward the flavor title.
- **Split option** — where the flavor could become two flavors that each still have 2–5 lessons (2.4), or "None".
- **People / Places** — who and where the reference layer links to the range, most-mentioned first.
- **Turn** — the words in the text where the section begins.

### 1. Verses: God So Loved the World
- **Range:** John 3:14–17; Romans 5:6–9; 1 John 4:6–9; 1 John 4:17–20; Jeremiah 31:1–4
- **Lessons:** 5
  1. John 3:14–17 — John 3:16
     - After saying the Son of man must be lifted up, the verse says “God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son”, so that believers have everlasting life (3:14, 3:16).
  2. Romans 5:6–9 — Romans 5:8
     - The letter tells its readers that “God commendeth his love toward us”: Christ died for us “while we were yet sinners” (5:6, 5:8).
  3. 1 John 4:6–9 — 1 John 4:8
     - Writing to the “Beloved” about loving one another, the letter says the one who does not love does not know God, “for God is love” (4:7–8).
  4. 1 John 4:17–20 — 1 John 4:19
     - Perfect love casts out fear, and “We love him, because he first loved us” (4:18–19).
  5. Jeremiah 31:1–4 — Jeremiah 31:3
     - The LORD speaks to Israel: “I have loved thee with an everlasting love”, and with lovingkindness He has drawn them (31:1, 31:3).
- **Split option:** At 1 John 4:8 (2 + 3 lessons).
- **People (3):** God, Jesus Christ, Moses
- **Places (0):** —
